## Who we are

iFINITY is an award-winning, London-based **iMIS AiSP and association specialist** with a global footprint. As a **Premier Authorised iMIS Solutions Provider (AiSP)** we partner with [Advanced Solutions International (ASI)](https://www.advsol.com/) — the makers of iMIS — to help associations, professional societies, unions, charities and other not-for-profits amplify engagement, streamline operations and maximise their impact.

With over 30 years' experience of delivering technology change in the membership world, we implement, integrate, extend and support **iMIS EMS**, alongside best-of-breed platforms including **TopClass LMS**, **OpenWater** and **Clowder**.

## What we build

### 🤖 AgentZ — AI for iMIS

**AgentZ** brings AI agents to iMIS EMS Cloud. Built on the open **Model Context Protocol (MCP)**, it exposes iMIS as a set of safe, purpose-built agent capabilities — membership, events, fundraising, billing, communications, content and RiSE website design, IQA reporting and query building, and system configuration — with preview-and-confirm guardrails on every change.

AgentZ is model-agnostic by design: it ships today as a Claude plugin, and the same toolset works with any MCP-capable AI assistant.

- 📦 [`ifinity-marketplace`](https://github.com/iFINITY-Ltd/ifinity-marketplace) — the public AgentZ plugin marketplace (releases and installation)
